# FortLocks

FortLocks is a minimal, immutable liquidity-locking protocol for Uniswap V3 on Ethereum.

It permanently locks Uniswap V3 position NFTs while allowing the trading fees generated by those positions to continue to be collected and distributed.

FortLocks V1 has no administrator, no upgrade mechanism, no emergency withdrawal, and no mechanism for releasing a successfully locked position.

## Core Properties

- Permanent Uniswap V3 liquidity locking.
- Canonical Ethereum mainnet Uniswap V3 NonfungiblePositionManager only.
- No unlock function.
- No liquidity withdrawal or decrease mechanism.
- No NFT transfer, approval, burn, migration, or recovery mechanism.
- No administrator or owner.
- No upgradeability or proxy.
- Permanent beneficiary selected when the position is locked.
- Permissionless fee collection.
- No dependency on a FortLocks frontend, API, database, or server.

## Fee Model

FortLocks charges:

- **0% of liquidity principal**
- **0.9% of post-lock collected trading fees**

The permanent beneficiary receives the remaining **99.1%** of collected post-lock fees.

Pre-existing tokens owed to the Uniswap V3 position at the time of locking are flushed directly to the beneficiary without a FortLocks fee.

Fee accounting carries fractional remainders forward between collections so splitting fees across many small permissionless collections cannot reduce the cumulative FortLocks fee.

## How Locking Works

A position owner approves FortLocks for a specific Uniswap V3 position NFT and calls:

`lock(tokenId, beneficiary)`

FortLocks then:

1. verifies that the caller owns the position;
2. records the permanent beneficiary;
3. transfers the position NFT into FortLocks;
4. flushes any pre-existing owed tokens directly to the beneficiary;
5. emits the `Locked` event.

After a successful lock, FortLocks contains no mechanism for transferring the position NFT back out or decreasing its liquidity.

Post-lock fees can be collected by calling:

`collectFees(tokenId)`

Anyone may trigger fee collection. The caller has no special entitlement to the collected funds and cannot choose or alter the recipients. Fees are always distributed to the position's permanent beneficiary and the immutable FortLocks fee recipient.

## Independent Verification

FortLocks is designed so that a lock can be independently verified directly from Ethereum.

Verification does not require trusting a FortLocks website, API, database, or FortLocks-operated RPC service.

The FortLocks V1 verification specification is available at:

`docs/FORT_V1_VERIFICATION.md`

The specification defines how to verify:

- the FortLocks V1 implementation;
- the official deployment;
- custody of a Uniswap V3 position NFT;
- the permanent beneficiary;
- the position's current Uniswap V3 liquidity;
- the absence of an unlock, administrator, or upgrade authority.

The canonical permanence statement is:

> **Once a canonical Uniswap V3 position NFT has been successfully locked in a verified FortLocks V1 contract, FortLocks V1 provides no mechanism to withdraw, transfer, approve, decrease, burn, migrate, recover, or otherwise release that position, and no administrator or upgrade authority exists that can add such a mechanism later.**

## Direct Contract Interaction

A FortLocks-specific frontend is not required.

Users and integrators can interact directly with the verified FortLocks contract using Ethereum tools such as Etherscan, Foundry Cast, or another compatible Ethereum interface.

The two primary state-changing functions are:

```solidity
lock(uint256 tokenId, address beneficiary)
collectFees(uint256 tokenId)
```

The permanent beneficiary for a position can be read from:

```solidity
locks(uint256 tokenId)
```

Position ownership and Uniswap V3 position data are independently available from the canonical Uniswap V3 NonfungiblePositionManager:

`0xC36442b4a4522E871399CD717aBDD847Ab11FE88`

## Security and Scope

FortLocks V1 deliberately has a narrow scope.

It assumes standard ERC-20 transfer behavior and does not attempt to provide special compatibility for fee-on-transfer, rebasing, blacklisting, or other non-standard token behavior.

FortLocks guarantees that FortLocks itself provides no mechanism for releasing a successfully locked Uniswap V3 position.

It does not guarantee the behavior, transferability, value, or continued operation of arbitrary underlying tokens, Uniswap, Ethereum, or other external systems.
